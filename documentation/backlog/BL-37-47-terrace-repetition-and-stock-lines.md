# Terrace: repetition, stock lines and reply pacing

## BL-47 — Residents replay their lines and reuse a refusal line
- **Fixed, part 1 (2026-09-30, `654db09`, live on beta): replayed introductions.** Hosted beta 2026-09-29: Yuriko and Hikaru said their authored arrival lines again, word for word, when greeted seven turns later. Cause: the authored intro is shown once, on the arrival turn (`opening_scene.py` `opening_arrival_segments`); the storyteller copied it back, and the repeat filter only looked at the last three messages. **New behavior:** a draft in which a character repeats one of their own earlier lines of 6+ words (read from that character's dialogue memories, so it reaches back the whole game) triggers the existing one-shot regeneration ("a character say again a line they already said", logged as `storyteller_repeat_regenerated` with `own_replay`); any replay still left is dropped, unless that would empty the reply. Short lines ("Goodnight, Paul-kun. Sleep well!") and narration are never touched; authored arrival beats are added afterwards and are unaffected. Code: `dialogue.py` `own_repeat_indexes`/`drop_own_repeated_lines`, `world_model/turn.py` `own_spoken_lines`, `prompt_engine.py`. Tests: `test_dialogue_own_repeats.py`, `world_model/test_own_spoken_lines.py`, `api/test_own_line_replay.py`. Live check: 14 turns with three "introduce yourselves again" prompts gave 0 replays in 67 lines, but the guard never fired in that run (0 regenerations logged), so the live result does not prove it by itself; the tests do.
- **Open, part 2: a refusal line is reused across unrelated turns.** Hosted beta 2026-09-29 (35 turns): after the first confession Minori answered many unrelated turns with variants of "I want to take my time getting to know everyone, no big plans for now" (moving rooms, breakfast, turn 26 while Paul made her breakfast); Hikaru reused a line Minori had just said. The exact-replay guard (`dialogue.py` `own_repeat_indexes`, checks each speaker's own earlier lines of 6+ words) does not catch variants. Mechanism unverified: either the refusal is re-injected each turn (`view.must_address`, relationship-decision memories in `romance.py`/`social_acts.py`) or the character's own earlier lines come back as retrieved dialogue memories.
- **Next:** build the prompt offline for a scripted state after a `not_yet` verdict and see whether the refusal or the speaker's old lines are injected on unrelated turns; pass the outcome only on the turn of the ask and when the player raises it again; failing-first test that a plain non-romantic message two turns after a `not_yet` carries no refusal directive. Measure by counting repeated sentences in a 25-turn replay.
